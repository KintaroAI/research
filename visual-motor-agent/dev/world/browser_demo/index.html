Controls

←→↑↓ or WASD set dx, dy = ±1. Space clicks once (one click per press). In mouse mode each step pulls the cursor toward the mouse, dx, dy = clip((mouse − cursor) / 4, −1, 1), and pressing the mouse button clicks. The world steps at a fixed rate whether or not you act, and waiting costs reward.
